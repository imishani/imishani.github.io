    Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University
    Advisor: Prof. Maxim Likhachev
    • Search-based planning with guarantees for high-dimensional and multi-robot manipulation: multi-graph search (MGS), constant-time planners that chain collision-free motion to manipulation behaviors (CTMP, B-CTMP; Best Paper, IROS 2026 workshop), and experience-accelerated multi-arm planning (xECBS; ICAPS 2024 Best Student Paper).
    • Integrating learning into planning: skill-centric manipulation planning with physics simulation and learned skill-effect world models (MOSAIC, Lazy-MOSAIC), diffusion-model planners for multi-robot motion (MMD; ICLR 2025 Spotlight), and parallel heuristic search as inference over actor-critic RL policies (PACHS).
    • Built and maintain SRMP (Search-Based Robot Motion Planning), motion planning software for single- and multi-manipulator systems with multiple physics-simulator backends, a MoveIt plugin for real robots, and an LLM-driven Agent Mode that sets up scenes, selects planners, and runs and debugs plans from natural language; 50K+ downloads.
